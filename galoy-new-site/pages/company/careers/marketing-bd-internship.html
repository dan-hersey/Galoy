Marketing & BD Internship Program

Internship · Remote · Marketing

About Galoy

Galoy develops financial infrastructure on Bitcoin, enabling institutions to offer financial services on the network. Our products include Lana (bitcoin-backed lending), Cala (core accounting ledger), Bria (payments bridge), the Blink wallet, and Stablesats.

About the Role

This internship is ideal for proactive, AI-knowledgeable students interested in marketing, business development, and Bitcoin. You'll collaborate with leadership to design and optimize marketing content and BD outreach using the latest AI tools.

Responsibilities

  • Trend Research — Research emerging AI-driven marketing tools and Bitcoin adoption trends
  • Growth Strategy — Use AI to identify opportunities and craft targeted messaging
  • Content Creation — Leverage AI to generate blog posts, newsletters, and social media content
  • Partnership Outreach — Deploy AI-assisted CRM tools for targeted BD campaigns
  • Performance Analysis — Track metrics, iterate on strategies, and report on campaign effectiveness

Requirements

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or related field
  • Strong interest in Bitcoin, fintech, and AI-driven marketing
  • Familiarity with AI tools such as ChatGPT, Midjourney, Copy.ai, Jasper, or HubSpot AI
  • Compelling writing skills and ability to tailor messaging to different audiences
  • Analytical and data-driven approach to marketing
  • Self-starter mentality suited to a startup environment

Nice to Have

  • SEO, email marketing, or social media growth experience
  • Bitcoin and Lightning Network knowledge
  • Banking technology familiarity
  • Spanish language proficiency

Benefits

  • Access to cutting-edge Bitcoin technology
  • Open-source contribution opportunities
  • Remote-first, diverse international team
  • Flexible compensation (fiat or Bitcoin)
  • Potential academic credit
