The Bitcoin Payments Opportunity

Traditional correspondent banking is slow, expensive, and complex. Bitcoin and Lightning offer instant settlement, transparent pricing, and borderless payments. Banks can now offer modern payment capabilities that customers demand.

24/7 Settlement

Bitcoin never sleeps. Payments settle instantly, any day, any time. No more weekend delays or banking hours constraints.

Cross-Border Payments

No correspondent banking. Send payments globally with minimal intermediaries and transparent fees.

Bring Your Custodian

Custody-independent architecture. Use your preferred provider or custody solution for your Bitcoin settlement.

How This Fits Your Institution

Bitcoin payments can be integrated at different depths depending on your institution's needs and readiness.

Light Integration

Add Bitcoin as a payment rail to your existing payment hub. Minimal changes to your infrastructure. Ideal for banks exploring Bitcoin payments as an additional offering.

Correspondent Banking Core

Use Bitcoin as a correspondent banking layer for cross-border payments. Full integration with your treasury and FX operations for seamless settlement.

Retail & Commercial Bank Core

Full-stack Bitcoin payment infrastructure for Bitcoin-first institutions. Native Bitcoin payments as a core banking service for all customer segments.
